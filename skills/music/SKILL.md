---
name: music
description: Play music and manage the Discord voice queue.
---
For song names or URLs use run_mod with name play and options {"query":"artist song"}. Pick the most likely match from the request. This is the only download implementation. The host joins the requester's voice channel if needed. Use queue_list, skip_audio, stop_audio and volume for queue controls. Downloads cap at 50 MiB and 20 minutes; media is deleted at track end. Do not download your own copy or build a permanent library. Report failures honestly.
